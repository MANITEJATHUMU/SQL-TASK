Question 1
Display the customer name, city, product, quantity, total amount (quantity × price) for customers who:
* belong to Hyderabad or Chennai
* purchased products between ₹2,000 and ₹70,000
* payment mode is UPI or Card
* order by total amount descending
* display only top 5 records.
select c.customer_id, c.customer_name, c.city, o.product, o.quantity,
       sum(o.quantity * o.price) as total_amount 
       from orders as o left join customers as c on  o.customer_id=c.customer_id
where city in ('Hyderabad' ,'Chennai') and price between 2000 and 70000 and payment_mode in ('UPI' ,'Card') 
group by  c.customer_id, c.customer_name,c.city,o.product,o.quantity
order by total_amount DESC
LIMIT 5;

Question 2
Display each category along with
* total sales
* average sales
* maximum sale
* minimum sale
* number of orders
Only include categories
* having total sales greater than ₹50,000
* average sales greater than ₹10,000
Sort by highest sales.
select category,
SUM(quantity*price) as total_sales,
avg(quantity*price) as avg_sales,
min(quantity*price) as min_sales,
max(quantity*price) as max_sales,
count(order_id) as total_orders 
from orders as o inner join customers as c on o.customer_id=c.customer_id
group by category
having 
SUM(quantity*price)>50000 and
avg(quantity*price)>10000;

Question 3
Find customers who have purchased more than one different product.
Display
* customer name
* city
* number of products purchased
* total quantity ordered
Sort by total quantity descending.
select 
c.customer_name,
c.city,
COUNT(DISTINCT o.product) as products,
SUM(o.quantity) as total_quantity 
from customers as c left join orders as o on c.customer_id=o.customer_id
group by c.customer_name,c.city
HAVING COUNT(DISTINCT o.product)>1 
order by SUM(o.quantity) DESC;

* city starts with 'H' or ends with 'i'

* customer name
* city
* product

Sort alphabetically by customer name.

Question 7
Find the top 3 customers who spent the highest amount.
Display
* customer name
* city
* total orders
* total quantity
* total spending
Only include customers
* having at least 2 orders.
select customer_name,
city,
COUNT(order_id) as total_orders,
SUM(quantity) as total_quantity,
SUM(quantity*price) as total_spending
from customers as c left join orders as o on c.customer_id=o.customer_id
group by customer_name,
city
having COUNT(quantity)>=2 order by total_spending DESC
limit 3;

Question 10 (Very Hard)
Prepare a sales report showing
* customer name
* city
* category
* total quantity
* total sales
* average order value
Only include customers
* whose age is between 25 and 40
* payment mode is Card or UPI
* category is Electronics or Furniture
Exclude 
* orders where quantity = 1
Display only categories
* having total sales greater than ₹50,000 
wSort by
1. Category
2. Total Sales DESC
3. Customer Name ASC
Display only the first 5 rows.
Select c.customer_name, c.city,o.category,
    SUM(o.quantity) AS total_quantity,
    SUM(o.quantity * o.price) AS total_sales,
    AVG(o.quantity * o.price) AS average_order_value
FROM customers AS c LEFT JOIN orders AS o ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id
WHERE c.age BETWEEN 25 AND 40  AND o.payment_mode IN ('Card', 'UPI') AND o.category IN ('Electronics', 'Furniture')  AND o.quantity <> 1
GROUP BY c.customer_name, c.city, o.category
HAVING  SUM(o.quantity * o.price) > 50000
ORDER BYo.category,total_sales DESC,c.customer_name ASC
LIMIT 5;
